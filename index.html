GymScheduleBot

Live demo ↗

An AI fitness planner that builds a weekly workout plan around your schedule and muscle recovery, then adds it to Google Calendar.

  • Idea to live appDesigned, built, and launched solo in October 2026
  • Calendar-awareSchedules workouts around existing commitments
  • Research-groundedPlans follow sports-science guidance on recovery

The problem

Most workout plans assume the same free time every week. Real schedules change, so people skip sessions or train the same muscles on back-to-back days.

What I built

  • A plan generator using Google's Gemini model that factors in each user's availability and muscle recovery.
  • A Google Calendar integration that places workouts in open time slots.
  • A Python (FastAPI) backend and HTML/CSS frontend, hosted on Render.

Stack

  • Python
  • FastAPI
  • Gemini API
  • Google Calendar API
  • HTML/CSS
  • Render

Is a UW Dorm Really That Expensive?

November 2026

I saw an Instagram post claiming UW has the most expensive dorms in the country, so I tested it. I compared UW Seattle with 500+ U.S. public universities using IPEDS, HUD rent, and CPI data to see whether the cost comes from Seattle's rent or from UW charging more.

  • UW's room charge ranks 2nd of 82 public R1 schools, and room and board grew 26% faster than inflation from 2014 to 2024
  • Regression shows local rent explains about a third of dorm prices (R² = 0.36, 537 schools)
  • Python
  • pandas
  • statsmodels
  • seaborn

Weather and Visitation at Mount Rainier

August 2026
INFO 201 · Team of 3

An INFO 201 team project asking whether weather at Paradise relates to monthly visits to Mount Rainier National Park in 2025, joining a year of hourly NWAC weather data with NPS visitor counts.

  • My part: cleaned and summarized the hourly weather data into monthly averages, built the weather charts, worked on the joined analysis, and outlined the presentation
  • Visits peak June to August, rising with daily high temperature and falling as precipitation increases, while humidity shows no seasonal link
